Master's Thesis - Predicting transport delays in courier logistics using machine learning

In recent years, machine learning–based predictive systems have become increasingly important within logistics and transportation research. There have been an increased interest in exploring data driven optimization approaches to improve service reliability.

Overview

This project is within the broader research area of applied machine learning in logistics and operations research. It will explore how historical operational data from Jetpak can be used to make predictions about transport delays and identify the underlying factors that most strongly influence performance.
